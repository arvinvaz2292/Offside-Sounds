Offside Sound App

by Arvin Vaz

How to Use

  1. Upload audio files.
  2. Play multiple tracks simultaneously.
  3. STOP fades tracks out smoothly over 2 seconds.
  4. STOP ALL fades everything safely.
  5. Playing tracks automatically move to the top.
  6. Use scene headings and notes during theatre cues.
  7. Designed for stable Chrome/iPad playback.

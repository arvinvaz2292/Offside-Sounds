Offside Sound App

by Arvin Vaz

How to Use

  1. Upload audio files.
  2. PLAY fades in over 3 seconds.
  3. STOP fades out over 3 seconds.
  4. Create loop regions directly on waveform.
  5. Drag loop edges visually.
  6. Playing tracks move to the top.
  7. Add scene headings and notes.
  8. Designed for theatre playback on iPad.

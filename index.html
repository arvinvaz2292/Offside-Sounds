📖 Offside Sound — How it works

Offside Sound is a theatre playback console designed for live operators. Built by Arvin Vaz. Below is everything you need to run a show.

🎬 Productions

Each show lives in its own Production. The picker on launch lets you choose one or create a new one. Switch between productions any time using the folder pill in the topbar. Each production keeps its own tracks, scenes, groups, and favourites — nothing leaks between shows.

🎵 Tracks

Tap ↑ UPLOAD to add audio. Choose Files for individual sounds or Folder to pull an entire show folder at once. Tracks auto-categorise into BGM (background music), SFX (sound effects), or AMB (ambience) based on their filename — you can change the category anytime by tapping its badge.

Each track has its own waveform, volume slider, play/stop, fade in / fade out (2-second ramp), and optional loop region + cue points. Volume slider behaves as a smooth fade; the ▲▼ buttons above the % give precise ±2% jumps with no fade.

🎭 Scenes

A scene is a snapshot of your current playback state: which tracks are playing, at what volumes, with what loops, plus which tracks are staged (ready but not auto-firing). Set up your tracks the way you want, then + Save current state as scene in the sidebar.

On each scene card:

  • ▶ FIRE — hard-cut to this scene
  • ⇄ XFADE — crossfade over the scene's fade duration (default 2s)
  • 👁 PREVIEW — see what's in the scene without playing it
  • ↑ UPDATE — overwrite the scene with your current state

Add the active scene's notes in the textarea below the scene. They show up in a banner above the track area when fired — useful for cue numbers, timings, or reminders.

⛬ Groups (stems)

A Group plays several stems in sample-accurate sync — drum kit, multitrack song, ambient layers. Tap + GROUP in the topbar, then on each track tap the dashed G+ button to add it to the group.

Inside the group container: one shared seek bar for all stems, individual volume sliders per stem (with mini-waveforms), group volume, fade in/out, and rewind. Hit play on the group and every stem fires at the same instant — no drift. Add a group to a scene just like a track, using the + SCENE button on the group's header.

⚙ Controls window

Draggable panel. Contains the Master fader, three category faders (BGM / SFX / AMB) with their own fade in/out, a Fade All Tracks button, and Favourites.

Favourites are one-shot sounds (door slams, switches, stings) that fire instantly at 50% on tap. They overlap freely. Upload short audio files via the + Add button.

▤ Global Mixer

The mixer shows every track and group in your production on one screen, with live volume sliders and play/stop on each row. Useful to see "what's actually happening" or to ride a gain in a hurry. Search box at the top filters by name.

🎚 Fader Board (second device)

You can use a second device — a phone, tablet, or another laptop — as a physical-style fader board for the show. It needs the local sync server running (see the FADER-BOARD-SETUP guide that came with the app). Once running, open the fader board URL on the second device.

The fader board gives you Master + BGM/SFX/AMB + a vertical fader for every track, each with a level meter and mute. It also has FIRE and XFADE buttons that advance through your scenes in order — so you can run the whole show from the second device while the console drives the audio. On a phone in landscape, faders spread across swipeable pages. If the network drops, the faders grey out and a warning shows — the console keeps working on its own regardless.

⌨ Keyboard shortcuts

  • 1-9 — trigger play/pause on tracks 1-9
  • Space — toggle the last-triggered track
  • S — fade all playing tracks to silence over 2 seconds
  • Esc — hard stop everything immediately

💾 Saving and backup

Everything auto-saves to your browser's storage every time you make a change. Export (sidebar footer) saves the production as a .json file you can email or back up. Import creates a new production from a .json file. Audio files don't get included in exports — you'll need to re-upload them, but smart-match restores them by filename.

📱 Running a show on iPad

  • Use USB-C → 3.5mm DAC adapter (Apple's works) → cable to mixer
  • Auto-Lock: Never — Settings → Display → Auto-Lock
  • Do Not Disturb: ON — no notifications mid-show
  • Guided Access — Settings → Accessibility — locks the iPad to just this app
  • Charge to 100% before doors; have a charger nearby
  • Don't pull-to-refresh the browser tab during a show — you'll lose loaded audio
  • Pre-play your first cue once during pre-show to warm up audio decode

🆘 If something goes wrong

  • Fade All (S key) for a quick controlled silence while you regroup
  • Esc for a hard stop on everything
  • If the app gets weird, close the tab and reopen it — the session is auto-saved

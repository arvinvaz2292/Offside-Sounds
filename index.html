Offside Sound App

by Arvin Vaz

How to Use

  1. Upload audio files.
  2. Press PLAY to fade tracks in over 3 seconds.
  3. Press STOP to fade tracks out over 3 seconds.
  4. Create draggable loop regions on the waveform.
  5. Enable LOOP to repeat the selected region.
  6. Playing tracks move to the top automatically.
  7. Add scene headings and cue notes.
  8. Designed for theatre playback on iPad.
